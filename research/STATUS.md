# Zeebo research status — v3.0

Updated: 15 November 2026.

The requested deeper pass has been performed across all 56 games and five applications. The 20 original reference/tool records remain available. This is a researched edition with explicit remaining gaps, not a claim that every surviving source has been exhausted. The shared editorial and UI decisions remain settled.

## What changed

The pass adds firsthand accounts of porting Quake II and Crash; Suzana Bueno’s account of Rolimã’s handling and local landmarks; Felipe Lira’s Super League recollection; Miguel Ángel Horna’s Data East emulation account; original regional reviews and player comments; and stronger release/edition distinctions. FIFA’s underlying Gamelion credits are separated from its Brazilian Zeebo adaptation team. A contaminated Need for Speed review and cross-platform GameFAQs reviews were checked and their unrelated material excluded.

Images now include creator portfolio galleries, Mônica interface artwork, a later developer portrait, original promotional-insert scans and a 2024 history-conference photograph. No verified 2009–2010 workplace photograph or original production sketch was retrieved; absence in this pass is not a claim of loss. Modern emulator captures remain explicitly labelled.

## Continuity records

- `coverage.json`: per-game/app work and remaining gaps.
- `searches/*.jsonl`: actual queries, reads, retrievals, access errors and research decisions, retained by researcher.
- `media-manifest.json`: published image attribution, source URLs and byte hashes.
- `access-limits.json`: image/retrieval obstacles and rejected substitutes.

The previous v2 pass did not retain a complete search history. Those inherited citations remain identified as earlier evidence; no retrospective query log has been fabricated. New logs include coverage notes and source assessments as well as searches, so their row counts must not be presented as independent searches or websites.

## Remaining gaps

No internal production account was recovered for several imported games, despite title, studio and named-creator searches. Zeebo App ID274791 remains unidentified beyond its separate inventory entry. Disney All Star Cards has an announced target launch but no newly verified actual launch date or named adaptation team. Exact Zenonia withdrawal/reissue notices remain inaccessible. Each game’s narrower limits are recorded in `coverage.json`; consult those before repeating searches or asserting the missing material does not exist.
