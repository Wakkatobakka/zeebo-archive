# Source handling

The complete source register is generated at `sources/index.html`, with links back to the relevant records. Each entry also has its own bibliography.

Original collection: https://archive.org/details/zeebo-romset-and-devtools

Development accounts include Tiago Tex Pine's portfolios for Zeebo Extreme, Foot Camp, Super League and Zeeboids; Michael Schade's October 2009 Galaxy on Fire interview; and Alexandre Nacari's Turma da Mônica project portfolio. Published credits and announcements supply further production and release details.

Original-game contributors and Zeebo adaptation staff retain their edition scope. Announcement dates and target release dates are distinguished from recorded releases. Quotations preserve the source wording and identify the speaker and publication.

The package includes 34 images across 13 game/app entries and three platform artifacts. These include creator-owned portfolio displays, game interfaces, a later developer portrait, an undated Portuguese console insert and a 2024 history-conference photograph. The original Infuse A1 emulator captures remain labelled. Capture dates are not inferred from portfolio publication dates. Each image retains a source URL, reproduction credit and SHA-256 in the research data and media manifest.

Actual searches and remaining gaps are recorded under `research/`. The earlier bibliography is not treated as a reconstructed search log. Mislabelled cross-platform reviews, mixed phone/console galleries and contaminated review paragraphs were explicitly screened.

The 14 printed-guide spreads remain external because their full-resolution archive is roughly 251 MB. Each guide reference retains the original archive URL and exact scan locator. No guide scan is silently substituted with an image from another platform.
