THE TELEVISION HAD MOBILE DNA

A console at the edge of two eras.

Zeebo brought a download-only, 3G-connected console to the television. The surviving collection brings together games, system applications, BREW development material and a printed game guide.

The numeric filenames are not the whole story. The 2011 OpenZeebo ID list restores their names, while the supplied guide gives 39 titles historical context.
