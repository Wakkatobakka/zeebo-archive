The game

A carrinho de rolimã is a small soapbox cart. This racer sends four selectable characters down courses named after São Paulo, Petrópolis and Ouro Preto. Gravity carries the carts downhill; players brake, drift and use turbo.[2][3]

Development

Rolimã opened the five-game Extreme series. Tiago Tex Pine led its game and interface design, alongside production of the series.[3][4]
