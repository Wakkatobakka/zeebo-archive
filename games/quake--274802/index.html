The game

Quake sends the player through military bases, medieval castles and lava-filled dungeons in search of four runes. Knights, ogres and other creatures stand in the way. Finding keys and hidden supplies is as much a part of reaching the exit as surviving the next fight.[2][3]

Development

The Zeebo adaptation retains credits for the original id Software team alongside a separate console production and programming group.[4]

Rafael Nanya and Heriberto Martinez Manrique are credited as the Zeebo chief engineers. The port’s programmers include Cesar Dantas de Castro, Ricardo Destro, Rafael Dazcal, Ademir José Barba and Mauricio Antonio Guerta. André Penha and Reinaldo Normand served as executive producers.[4]

Release and reception

Quake and Quake II were removed from ZeeboNet in June 2010. The free-download offer switched to Zeebo Extreme Rolimã and Jetboard.[5]
