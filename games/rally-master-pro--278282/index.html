The game

Rally Master Pro offers four modes across courses with different surfaces. The Zeebo guide lists asphalt, stones, sand and snow.[2]

Development

Fishlabs developed and published the game. It began on Java phones in 2008, then reached Zeebo in Brazil in December 2009, with Tectoy handling distribution.[3]
