Tork and Kral: A Prehistorik Adventure

Two cavemen clear creature-filled platform stages with clubs, fire and ice.[2]

The game

A scientist named Ragor travels into the past and kidnaps Kama. Tork and Kral pursue him through 60 levels, fighting dinosaurs, bears and other creatures with clubs. Fire and ice upgrades let the cavemen trap enemies before finishing them off.[2]

The game supports two players working together. Its Escape mode changes the objective to climbing fast enough to stay ahead of the scrolling screen.[2]

Development

Vega Mobile developed Tork and Kral under the earlier name Ice and Rock. In a 24 September 2010 report based on contact with the studio, ZeeboBrasil named Jerome Schurch as producer and reported that the game was awaiting Zeebo’s approval.[3]
