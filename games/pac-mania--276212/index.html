The game

Pac-Mania moves PAC-MAN’s dot collecting into an isometric maze. Clear the dots to reach the next round, use power pellets to turn the tables on the ghosts, and jump over pursuers when the route is blocked. Some ghosts can jump too.[2][3]

Development

Namco released the arcade original in 1987. Tomoyuki Shimizu designed the game, with Shimizu and Toru Iwatani credited as directors. The Zeebo release record names Namco Bandai Games as the port developer and publisher.[3][4]
