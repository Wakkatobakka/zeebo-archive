The game

Street Hoop plays basketball with three-player teams. Matches emphasize quick attacks, high jumps and exaggerated slam dunks.[2]

Two action buttons cover shooting, passing, blocking and steals. Successful baskets fill a power meter for exaggerated special shots. The Zeebo release supports two-player matches, but movement relies on the D-pad rather than the analog stick.[3]

Development

G-Mode’s March 2010 announcement used the name Street Slam. Onan Games ported the Data East game to Zeebo.[4][5]

The Zeebo Data East releases used emulation.[6]

Release and reception

The Brazilian Zeebo edition is dated 16 April 2010. The game also appears in international catalogs as Street Slam and Dunk Dream.[5]

STX’s April 2010 review praised its pace and reported no noticeable lag. He liked the music and competitive replay value, while criticising the lack of analog support on the Z-Pad. Even though he did not usually enjoy basketball games, he kept wanting another match.[3]
