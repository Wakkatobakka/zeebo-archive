Alien Breaker Deluxe

Bounce a ball through alien formations and collect paddle upgrades.[2]

Developer and publisher

The game

Alien Breaker Deluxe uses a paddle and ball to clear formations of alien blocks. Some blocks take several hits; others release bonuses that change the paddle, add weapons or protect the bottom of the screen.[2]

Arcade and Challenge cover 100 levels across ten zones. Versus gives each player a separate playfield, while Fronton has two players competing on the same screen.[2]

Development

The Zeebo version followed Vega Mobile’s work on PopCap’s catalog. Its own arcade campaign doubled the iPhone game’s 50 stages to 100. STX’s contemporary review distinguished that expansion from a simple unchanged conversion and tested the new edition’s controls and multiplayer modes.[3]

Release and reception

The Zeebo edition was released on 20 May 2010.[2]

In a 28 May 2010 review, STX praised the bright 2D art and the response of both the D-pad and left analogue stick. The main complaint was sound: after the title-screen music, play continued with effects and an English-speaking item announcer rather than a musical backing. The reviewer particularly recommended playing with another person on the same screen.[3]
