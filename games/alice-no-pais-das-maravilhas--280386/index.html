The game

Alice can cross between Wonderland and its mirror world. The White Rabbit alters time, the Cheshire Cat reveals hidden objects, the March Hare moves them, and the Mad Hatter sends them between worlds. Reaching the next area means working out whose ability to use and where.[2]

Development

Disney Interactive Studios Beijing developed the film tie-in. Zeebo Interactive Studios handled the console adaptation, led by Danny Caldas with producer Hugo Faria. The credits also name Olímpio MacLouly and Thomas Gomes as developers, and Denis Koishi as musician.[3]

Release and reception

Alice was part of the Disney Mobile lineup announced for Zeebo in September 2010. The game also appeared under the English title Alice in Wonderland: An Adventure Beyond the Mirror.[4][5]
