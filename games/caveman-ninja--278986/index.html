The game

Joe and Mac fight dinosaurs and members of a rival tribe in a prehistoric platform game. They can attack up close or throw weapons at enemies beyond their clubs’ reach.[2][3]

Development

Onan Games adapted Data East’s game for Zeebo. G-Mode announced Caveman Ninja with Spinmaster and Street Slam in March 2010.[4][5]

Release and reception

Release records date the Mexican edition to 31 March 2010 and the Brazilian edition to 16 April.[4]
