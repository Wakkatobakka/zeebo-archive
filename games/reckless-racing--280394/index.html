The game

Reckless Racing follows cars around off-road tracks from an overhead viewpoint. Pixelbite developed the game.[2]

Development

Reckless Racing was Pixelbite’s third game and its first project that was not work-for-hire. The studio had wanted to make an old-fashioned top-down racer with the detail and feel of a console game. Its portfolio credits Instant Remedy’s banjo soundtrack, alongside the bumps, slides and vehicle differences that made the racing distinctive.[3]

It first appeared in the announced Zeebo lineup as Deliverace. By the November launch, the name had changed to Reckless Racing. Polarbit published the console version, while EA handled the contemporary iPhone and iPad release.[4][5]

Release and reception

The official Zeebo blog announced availability on 3 November 2010, less than two weeks after the iPhone/iPad launch. It specifically told owners to use the wired controller. That instruction distinguishes the console controls from the tilt and touchscreen options promoted on phones.[5]
