The game

Prey Evil belongs to the mobile retelling of Prey. Tommy searches for his missing girlfriend Jenny, fighting with a wrench, rifle and organic grenades. The adventure is divided into chapters and discrete levels. Spirit Walk lets him leave his body to solve obstacles; defeat can send him into a spirit realm to recover health and power.[2]

Development

The Zeebo credits name Seth A. Robinson as lead programmer, Andreas Vasen as producer and Scott Miller as executive producer. Les Pardew and Weston Tracy are credited for art.[3]

Seth A. Robinson had already programmed MachineWorks’ BREW games, including Duke Nukem Mobile 3D and Rocky Balboa 3D. His project history lists Prey Evil for Zeebo and Prey Invasion for iPhone as separate 2009 assignments.[4]

Before launch, John Rizzo estimated that the Zeebo adaptation was taking roughly seven months.[5]

QSound’s June 2009 financial statement confirms that Prey Evil licensed its Ripp3D graphics engine. The free-download arrangement paid QSound a royalty for every console sold in Brazil; the company also discussed additional games as future plans.[6]

Release and reception

In June 2009, QSound said Zeebo purchasers were entitled to download Prey Evil free of charge.[6]

Claudio Prandoni’s launch report judged Prey’s movement and actions unusually responsive among the first Zeebo games. He also found some levels little more than straight corridors with a few enemies.[7]
