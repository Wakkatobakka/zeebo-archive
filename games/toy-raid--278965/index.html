The game

Toy Raid puts the player behind a cannon, defending a base from toy aircraft and parachuting soldiers. Bombs threaten the base from above; soldiers who land can attack it from inside. Power-ups add guided bullets, scatter shots and other weapons.[2]

Toy Raid was Flying Tiger’s first Zeebo project. John Brandstetter described its origins:[3]

The Zeebo credits name Michael Fernie for programming, Jeremy Klein for art and Brian Twist for testing.[2]

Release and reception

Toy Raid launched for Zeebo in March 2010. A contemporary company announcement described it as Flying Tiger’s first game for the system.[3]
