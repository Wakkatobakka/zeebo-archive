The game

Armageddon Squadron is an arcade flight game. Its missions send the player against air and ground targets in a fictional Second World War setting.[2]

Development

Polarbit developed the game and announced a Zeebo version in April 2010.[3][4]

People

Studios and production

  • PolarbitDeveloper and publisher[3]

Release and reception

The Zeebo release reached Brazil in September 2010, distributed by Zeebo Brasil.[3]
