The game

Queimada is dodgeball. Its teams have four players, and the Boomerang version uses swinging motions to throw or dodge while characters move automatically. Buttons handle passing, catching and taunting.[2]

The Portuguese guide also advertises new character outfits to unlock.[3]

Passing and feinting are central to attacks. In the original Boomerang version, button 1 lets a player fake a throw, while button 2 passes to a teammate. Without the ball those buttons taunt and attempt a catch; a motion upward dodges. Two camera views offer a different look at the court.[4]

Development

Hugo Faria produced the game. Bruno Evangelista and Tiago Monteiro Fernandes led programming, Seigi Sato led art, and Denis Koishi supplied music.[2]

Release and reception

The original Boomerang Sports Queimada was released on 5 February 2010. Its Zeebo Sports relaunch added Z-Pad controls in August 2010; two-player games required matching controller types.[2][5]

STX’s 19 April 2010 Zeebo Club review found the instructions more intimidating than the controls turned out to be. He praised the growing challenge and local multiplayer, but thought the street noises and lack of music became monotonous. It received 7/10, with the reviewer explicitly recommending it to Boomerang owners.[4]
