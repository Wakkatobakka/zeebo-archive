The game

Dark Seal offers four heroes with different weapons: a knight, bard, magician and ninja. Alongside their ordinary attacks, spells and transformations give them other ways to fight through the monsters.[2]

The isometric view allows movement in eight directions. Killing enemies fills a magic meter while a book cycles through available spells. Casting at the right moment transforms the character, with forms including a tornado, a Medusa head and a giant rat accompanied by smaller rats. Treasure can temporarily improve speed, strength or defence.[3]

Development

The Zeebo port was made by Onan Games from Data East’s original. G-Mode published it.[4]

The Zeebo Data East releases used emulation.[5]

Release and reception

The Brazilian Zeebo release is dated 4 May 2010. International records also list the game as Gate of Doom.[4]

A contemporary comparison survives in STX’s Heavy Barrel review. He described Dark Seal and Wizard Fire as earlier Data East releases limited to the Z-Pad’s D-pad, contrasting them with the later analog-enabled Heavy Barrel and Bad Dudes.[6]
