The game

Karnov’s Revenge is a one-on-one fighting game. Pick a fighter and combine normal attacks and special moves to defeat the rest of the roster.[2]

The Zeebo version uses the D-pad rather than analog movement. Its pause-menu help lists the fighters’ moves, so players can check a command during a match. STX pointed readers towards that built-in list when requests for a separate move guide appeared beneath his review.[3]

Development

Onan Games made the Zeebo port of Data East’s fighter. It was part of G-Mode’s announced 2010 Data East lineup.[4][5]

The Zeebo Data East releases used emulation.[6]

Release and reception

Listed internationally as Fighter’s History Dynamite, it arrived in Mexico on 13 April 2010 and Brazil on 16 April.[4]

STX reviewed it two days after the recorded Brazilian release, while players were still waiting for the promised Street Fighter Alpha. He criticised the Z-Pad’s D-pad and uneven animation, but enjoyed returning to its difficult arcade fights. He awarded 7.5/10 for veteran players and 7/10 for newcomers.[3]
