The game

Vôlei covers serving, attacks, blocks and defensive returns. Characters move automatically, while the player times actions with the controller. One or two players choose doubles teams, and victories earn points towards costumes.[2][3]

The Boomerang’s directional pad changes the camera; its buttons handle jumping and blocking.[3]

Development

Hugo Faria produced Vôlei. Bruno Evangelista and Tiago Monteiro Fernandes led programming; Seigi Sato led art, with Paula Carneiro Amin, Pedro Hamaya and Carina Cheng among the artists.[3]

Its voice cast included Rafael Marques, Cacau Pastorello, Danny Caldas, Suellen Carollo and Carina Cheng, alongside Denis Koishi’s music.[3]

Release and reception

Vôlei was released on 14 December 2009 as Boomerang Sports Vôlei. The August 2010 Zeebo Sports relaunch added Z-Pad controls while keeping the same game.[3][4]

The later games prompted comparisons with Vôlei. In his April 2010 Queimada review, STX preferred Vôlei’s music to the newer game’s street ambience. His May Peteca review described a related motion-control approach, with the difference that a timed swing could make the Peteca player jump without an extra button press.[5][6]
