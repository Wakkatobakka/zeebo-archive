The game

Iron Sight gives each side time to move and aim before firing. Players fight for the Corporation or the Rebellion, using missiles and items such as teleporters. Holding the fire button increases a shot’s range; releasing it launches the weapon.[2]

The Zeebo campaign contains eight levels with different terrain and enemy positions. A multiplayer mode offers another way to play.[3]

Development

The Zeebo credits name Deion Mobile as the developer and list five authors: Tomek Wagner, Raymond Leung, Bartek Tajer, Vyach Imalkovski and Justin Wasack.[4]

Polarbit’s Zeebo agreement covered four titles, with Iron Sight and Armageddon Squadron forming the first wave. By 30 September 2010 both were available in Brazil and Mexico.[5]

Release and reception

Polarbit brought Iron Sight to Zeebo. It was available in Brazil and Mexico by the end of September 2010.[5]

STX’s November 2010 review found responsive movement but troublesome aiming: the player could adjust trajectory and force, yet the fixed view made depth difficult to judge. The review also noted that music heard in the menu disappeared during battle. Despite those complaints, its campaign and multiplayer could still hold the attention of a player who liked turn-based combat.[3]
