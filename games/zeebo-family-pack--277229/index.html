The game

Family Pack lets players choose a character and compete in seven games: 8ball, Cannonball, Dirtbike, Formula 0.01, Long John Silver, Score and Zap. The Zeebo version supports one or two players.[2][3]

Development

Jadestone’s credited team had six people. Tommy Palm produced the collection, Alexander Ekvall led development, Henrik Pettersson was lead illustrator and Lina Hansson directed the art. Patrik Lindegren and Erik Olsson are the other developers named.[4]

Release and reception

Family Pack arrived on Zeebo on 29 September 2009. Its seven games were subsequently released individually for mobile devices.[2]
