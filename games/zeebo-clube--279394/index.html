The application

Zeebo Clube was a free application for following news from Zeebo without leaving the console. The company described release announcements, promotions and other information about the system.[2]

People

Studios and production

  • Zeebo BrasilApplication operator and distributor[2]

Release and reception

Zeebo Brasil introduced the application on its official blog on 3 September 2010 and directed owners to install it from the console’s store. The post said availability for everyone would follow.[2]
