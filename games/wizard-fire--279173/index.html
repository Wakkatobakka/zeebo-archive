The game

Wizard Fire sends five fantasy heroes through a kingdom filled with monsters, weapons, magic and treasure. It can be played alone or with a second player.[2][3]

Development

Onan Games ported Data East’s game to Zeebo. G-Mode announced it in March 2010 under the spelling Wizard of Fire.[4][5]

The Zeebo Data East releases used emulation.[6]

Release and reception

Wizard Fire was released for Zeebo in Brazil on 16 April 2010.[4]

Ahtrun reviewed Wizard Fire the day after its recorded Brazilian release. He enjoyed the cooperative play and reported no lag, but the Z-Pad’s D-pad was a major obstacle. The unchanged English story also made it less accessible to the Brazilian audience the console was meant to reach.[3]
