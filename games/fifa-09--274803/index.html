The game

FIFA 09 on Zeebo offers four modes: a friendly match for one or two players, ten set challenges, a full season in one of eleven leagues, and a choice of ten tournaments. Players can change their lineup and roles, while a stamina meter limits continuous running.[2]

Development

Gamelion developed this edition for Electronic Arts. Its credits name Krzysztof Jędruczyk as development lead and Hubert Borowy and Sebastian Szczygieł as producers. Wojciech Lekki handled 3D art.[2]
