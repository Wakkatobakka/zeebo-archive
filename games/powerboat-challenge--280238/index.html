The game

Powerboat Challenge offers four pilots with boats that have different handling traits. A tutorial explains steering and passing the buoys. Completing enough events opens the next island, with conventional races and elimination events among the challenges.[2]

Money earned from events buys improvements for the boat, while medals unlock island championships. Passing close to the correct side of a buoy replenishes turbo; missing a gate costs speed and can eventually disqualify the racer.[3]

Development

Fishlabs developed the game. The Zeebo credits name Lars Berger and Stefan Görlich as lead developers, Henning Lubahn for 3D and level design, and Gero Goerlich for music and sound effects. Jörg Thomaschewski produced it.[4]

The Zeebo edition supported the Boomerang as well as the Z-Pad. STX found that motion-control play used automatic acceleration, with the Boomerang’s directional buttons handling braking and turbo. The Z-Pad offered more precise control, while the Boomerang changed the feel of steering.[3]

Release and reception

Powerboat Challenge launched on 12 October 2010, Brazilian Children’s Day. The official post encouraged players to earn money and customise their boats.[5]

STX’s November review praised the environments and Boomerang support, but found occasional stutters and a conspicuous lack of engine noise. The race music was a rock-and-drum-and-bass mixture rather than an engine soundtrack. Local multiplayer was absent.[3]
