The game

Vamos Brincar nº 1 combines counting, colours, jigsaws, memory games and mazes. Anjinho needs particular balloons popped; Magali wants matching fruit found; Mônica helps Cascão cross a river and searches for Sansão. Marina’s painting activity also includes a free drawing tool.[2]

There are three difficulty levels and 30 achievements. Players can import a Zeeboid to keep track of their achievements.[2]

Development

The game was made by Zeebo Interactive Studios in Campinas with Mauricio de Sousa Productions. Their November 2010 licensing announcement proposed nine educational and entertainment titles, including activity centres and games about maths and language.[3]

Artist Alexandre Nacari identifies animation, assets and the graphical interface as his contributions. His portfolio names Flash and Photoshop as the tools he used for this 2010 project.[4]

Release and reception

The Brazilian Zeebo game was released on 21 December 2010. Its published title is also recorded as Turma da Mônica em Vamos Brincar nº 1; this archive retains OpenZeebo’s “Vol. 1” label.[2][1]
