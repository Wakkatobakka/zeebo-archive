The game

Blade and Striker are recruited to rescue President Ronnie from the DragonNinja clan. Bad Dudes is a side-scrolling brawler: the two street fighters punch and kick their way through the kidnappers.[2]

Enemies usually fall after a single hit, while bosses can take more punishment. Knives, nunchucks and other dropped weapons extend the fighters’ limited moves. Two players can fight together.[3]

Development

Data East created the original game. Onan Games handled the Zeebo port, published by G-Mode.[4]

The Zeebo Data East releases used emulation.[5]

Release and reception

The Brazilian Zeebo release is dated 27 July 2010, alongside Heavy Barrel.[4][6]

STX’s August 2010 review gave it 3.7/10. The dark graphics and harsh enemy voices drew particular criticism. Analog movement was a welcome addition after earlier Data East releases had relied on the Z-Pad’s D-pad, but STX still found the jumps and attacks awkward.[3]
