Bejeweled Twist

Rotate four gems at a time to build matches.[2]

The game

Bejeweled Twist changes the usual gem swap into a rotation. Turning a group of coloured stones lines up matches and can set off further combinations.[2]

Development

The Zeebo credits distinguish the original design by Brian Fiete, Jason Kapalka and Kurt Pfeifer from the later engineering and production teams. Vincent McCreith is credited for music on this edition.[3]
