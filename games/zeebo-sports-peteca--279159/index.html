The game

Peteca uses a shuttlecock struck with the hand. In the Boomerang version, the character moves around the court automatically and the player swings the controller to return it. The guide advertises unlockable outfits and courts.[2][3]

Development

Hugo Faria produced Peteca, with Bruno Evangelista leading programming. Its artists included Bruno Carias Fogaça, Paula Carneiro Amin, Suellen Carollo, Luiz Gustavo Marquetti and Seigi Sato.[2]

Release and reception

Peteca first appeared as Boomerang Sports Peteca in May 2010. On 17 August, Zeebo announced its relaunch as Zeebo Sports Peteca with Z-Pad support.[2][4]
