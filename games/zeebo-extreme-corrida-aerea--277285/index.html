The game

Corrida Aérea has three circuits raced over three laps. Flying through rings refills the aircraft’s turbo. The Portuguese guide places its canyons, caves, bridges and tunnels in Chapada Diamantina.[2][3]

Development

The Extreme games and their tools were written in C++ using BREW APIs. The artists used additional 3ds Max scripts to export their work into the engine.[4]
