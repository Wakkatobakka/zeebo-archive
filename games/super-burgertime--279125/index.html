The game

Peter Pepper Jr. and his twin brother Pete assemble burgers by jumping on their ingredients. Each layer drops toward a serving plate, with food enemies chasing the cooks around the platforms and ladders. Falling ingredients can crush those enemies.[2]

The pepper shaker is the starting weapon, with other weapons and items appearing at the edge of the screen. Attacks can be directed up or down while on a ladder. Jumping, attacking and dropping burger layers have to be balanced against a constant supply of new enemies.[3]

Development

Data East released the arcade original in 1990. Its credits name Koji Akibayashi as game designer, Mario Watanabe and Yoshiyuki Urushibara as graphic designers, and Tatsuya Kiuchi and Tomoyoshi Sato for sound. Onan Games made the Zeebo port.[2][4]

The Zeebo Data East releases used emulation.[5]

Release and reception

G-Mode announced Super Burger Time for Zeebo in March 2010. The Brazilian release is dated 16 April.[6][7]
