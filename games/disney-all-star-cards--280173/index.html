The game

Disney All Star Cards brings together six card games. Each table has one human player and three computer opponents. Pixie dust can buy cards and gallery artwork, or be spent on a game-specific cheat.[2]

Studios and production

Release and reception

Disney Mobile announced the Zeebo edition on 1 September 2010, with a target launch date of 21 September.[3]
