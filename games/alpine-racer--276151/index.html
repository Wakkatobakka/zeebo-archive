The game

Alpine Racer offers downhill skiing and snowboarding. Players choose a character, race through snowy courses and perform aerial tricks.[2]

Development

This Alpine Racer came from Namco’s mobile lineup. Its BREW edition appeared in 2008, followed by iPhone and Zeebo versions in 2009. Namco Bandai Games is credited as developer, with Namco Networks publishing and Tectoy distributing the Zeebo release.[3]
