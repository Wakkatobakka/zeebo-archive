The game

Super League turns football into an arcade game with superpowers. Players create a team and take it through a career, using their Zeeboids as footballers.[2][3]

Development

Tiago Tex Pine led a team of fifteen and designed systems and power-ups. He describes the game as the result of three years working with Zeebo, during which the team revised its custom engine and built tools to get more from the Adreno graphics hardware.[2]

Art direction was handled by Marcelo Marcati, with concept art by Rafael Marquetto and Wilson Hideki Akamine. Élcio Faria Ribeiro wrote the script and also worked in QA.[3]

Release and reception

Super League was released on 22 December 2010 as Zeebo’s main Christmas title that year. Its Portuguese title is Zeebo F.C. Super Liga.[2][3]
