The game

Magical Drop III puts the player beneath a descending field of coloured balls. Pull down balls of one colour, move sideways and throw them back to complete matches. The Zeebo controls use button 1 to collect balls, button 2 to return them and button 3 to bring the ball field down.[2]

Survival lasts as long as the player can keep up. Challenge pits a chosen character against successive opponents. Magical Journey adds a board-game route with separate challenges, items and bosses. Two players can compete directly.[2]

Development

Onan Games handled the Zeebo port of Data East’s puzzle game. G-Mode included it in its March 2010 announcement.[3][4]

The Zeebo Data East releases used emulation.[5]

Release and reception

The Brazilian Zeebo release is dated 22 April 2010.[3]

STX’s April 2010 review awarded 8.5/10, praising the animation, presentation and responsive controls. The pace distinguished it from the more relaxed Bejeweled Twist: there was little time to plan while the balls kept descending. Repeated character voices were one of his reservations.[2]
