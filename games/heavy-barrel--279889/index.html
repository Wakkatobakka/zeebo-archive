The game

Heavy Barrel is an overhead shooter set in a nuclear missile installation seized by terrorists. Its most powerful weapon was dismantled and locked away: the player collects keys and recovers its six parts while fighting through the base. Two players can tackle the mission together.[2]

Development

The original Data East arcade game appeared in 1987. Its credits name Koji Akibayashi for game design and Azusa Hara and Hiroaki Yoshida for music and sound. Onan Games later handled the Zeebo port.[2][3]
