The game

Peggle turns each shot into a bouncing path through a field of pegs. Its ten masters supply different powers for clearing the orange targets.[2]

Development

Vega Mobile handled the Zeebo conversion. Its credit roll preserves Brian Rothstein and Sukhbir Sidhu’s original concept separately from the production, engineering and art roles, then explicitly identifies Vega as the console-version studio. This was part of the same PopCap adaptation work that brought Heavy Weapon to Zeebo.[3]

Release and reception

Peggle was available on Zeebo by 9 March 2010. PopCap’s announcement lists 55 main levels, ten unlockable masters, Quick Play, Duel, and a further 55 Challenge levels.[4]

STX’s May 2010 review found a cheerful game with more tactical appeal than its appearance initially suggested. Both the D-pad and analogue stick could aim, although the analogue movement sometimes became excessively fast. Large chains of hits could also cause slowdown. The turn-taking Duel mode was a particular attraction for local play.[5]
