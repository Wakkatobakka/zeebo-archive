The game

Foot Camp separates football into penalties, free kicks, dribbling and keep-ups. The free-kick game adds wind and a defensive wall; dribbling asks players to repeat button sequences. Power-ups can turn a goalkeeper into a chicken or have aliens abduct opponents.[2]

Development

Tiago Tex Pine designed Foot Camp for friends playing together. Three activities use turns and score comparisons, while Goal Scoring allows direct, simultaneous competition. He led a twelve-person team and worked on the game design, interface wireframes, balancing and user testing.[2]

The game used C++, BREW and Adreno SDKs, with an internal OpenGL ES engine developed by Tectoy Digital and Zeebo Studios.[2]

Release and reception

Zeebo announced Foot Camp and Zeeboids together on 3 June 2010 for Brazil and Mexico. Zeebo Interactive Studios in Brazil created both. The announcement described groups of up to eight taking turns.[3]
