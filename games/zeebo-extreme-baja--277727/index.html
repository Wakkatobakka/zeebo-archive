The game

Baja races small off-road cars across dirt tracks, caves and rough ground. Jumps interrupt the course, and collected fuel supplies a turbo boost.[2][3]

Development

Tiago Tex Pine led game design and production across the five Extreme games. He described the work involved:[4]

The team grew to 23 while making the series during 2009, working towards a schedule of one finished game every two months.[4]
