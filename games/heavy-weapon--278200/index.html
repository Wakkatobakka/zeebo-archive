The game

Heavy Weapon is a side-scrolling tank shooter with enemies attacking from land, sea and air. Equipment upgrades are available between stages.[2]

Development

Vega Mobile made the Zeebo version from an existing mobile adaptation. The credits name Wayne Imlach, René Laurent, Eric Thommerot and Philip Plunkett on that earlier work, with Viktorya Hollings as executive producer of the Zeebo edition.[3]
