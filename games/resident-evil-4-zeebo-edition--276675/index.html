The game

Resident Evil 4 Zeebo Edition breaks Leon’s rescue mission into twelve chapters, joined by cutscenes. It keeps the over-the-shoulder view and requires him to stop moving before he fires. The Merchant sells weapons and items using money earned during play.[2]

Alongside its story, the console edition offers 24 Mercenary missions, unlocked two at a time through the twelve story missions, to earn money for equipment. Completing the story unlocks Professional mode; starting a new round retains money and weapons while resetting mission progress.[3]

Development

The Zeebo release credits Ideaworks3D as developer, Capcom as publisher and Tectoy as distributor. HI Corporation separately confirmed that this version used its MascotCapsule eruption technology.[4][5]

The result is a chapter-based adaptation rather than the continuous console adventure. On-screen action prompts handle interactions such as placing ladders, kicking stunned enemies and evading boss attacks. The twelve-mission story and extra money-earning challenges change the rhythm of progression.[3]

Release and reception

Resident Evil 4 and Ridge Racer appeared in the Brazilian catalog in August 2009. Cido Coelho’s contemporary report priced RE4 at 2490 Z-Credits, or R$24.90.[6]

Mig64’s March 2010 review tried to assess the Zeebo release separately from the expectations attached to its name. The writer found the movement smooth and some boss fights demanding, but criticised the subdued sound, bright presentation and short or static missions. The conspicuous blue Ganados became part of the argument about the adaptation.[3]
