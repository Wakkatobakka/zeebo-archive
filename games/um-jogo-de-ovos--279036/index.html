The game

Toto wants to become a chicken. Confi retells that journey in an alternative story based on Una Película de Huevos, with Toto and Willy as playable characters and a third character to unlock.[2]

The game has 25 stages, five boss fights and ten unlockable competition stages. Two players can cooperate or race each other.[2]

Development

Fabrication Games developed the game, with Tommy Palm directing and Alexander Ekvall leading development and game design. David Telenius designed the levels, Henrik Pettersson led the art, and Oskar Rahm worked on 3D art and animation.[3]

Huevocartoon’s team supplied the film characters and cutscenes. Jarpa Studio is credited for sound production.[4]

Release and reception

Un Juego de Huevos launched in Mexico on 19 April 2010. Brazil received it as Um Jogo de Ovos on 24 August 2010.[4]
