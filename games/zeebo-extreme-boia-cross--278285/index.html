The game

Bóia Cross sends its riders down rapids in inner tubes. Energy drinks replenish the boost, and the guide advertises special tricks. Its three courses are named Santo André, Bonito and Caverna do Diabo.[2][3]

Development

Tiago Tex Pine led design across the Extreme series. Level layouts were worked out with the 3D environment artists and QA staff.[4]

Release and reception

Bóia Cross was released for Zeebo on 21 December 2009. It is also known as Zeebo Extreme Acqua Ride.[3]
