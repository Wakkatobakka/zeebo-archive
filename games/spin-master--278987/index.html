The game

Johnny and Tom set off on a treasure hunt after a kidnapping. Their weapons include a magical yo-yo, used to fight through the platform stages.[2]

Development

Spin Master was among the first three Data East games announced for Zeebo in March 2010. Onan Games made the port for publisher G-Mode.[3][4]

Studios and production

  • Data East CorporationOriginal game developer · Original arcade edition[4]
  • Onan GamesPort developer[4]

Release and reception

Release records list Spinmaster in Mexico on 31 March 2010 and Brazil on 16 April.[4]
