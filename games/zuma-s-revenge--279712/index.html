The game

Zuma’s Revenge returns to the ball-shooting puzzle game with hostile spirits and tiki bosses. Matching colours clears sections of the advancing chains.[2]

The Zeebo edition has 60 levels and an additional challenge mode. Advancing chains leave little time to line up groups of three matching balls, and special shots can help stop the board from filling.[3]

Development

Vega Mobile ported PopCap’s game to Zeebo. Reflective Audio is credited for music.[4]

Release and reception

The Brazilian Zeebo release followed in July 2010.[4]

Ahtrun’s review appeared on 17 July 2010. It praised the long campaign, demanding stages and musical atmosphere, while finding the graphics comparatively lifeless and noting occasional sound interruptions and delayed controls. The review awarded 8/10. Its comments also show disagreement among owners: some welcomed the challenge while others wanted a wider variety of releases.[3]
