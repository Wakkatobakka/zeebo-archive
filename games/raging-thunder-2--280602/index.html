The game

Raging Thunder 2 is an arcade street-racing game. Polarbit announced it for Zeebo alongside Armageddon Squadron, Iron Sight and Deliverace.[2]

Development

Pixelbite includes Raging Thunder 2 in its own game portfolio. GameFAQs credits Pixelbite as developer of the Zeebo edition and Polarbit as publisher; Polarbit’s contemporary announcement confirms the console release.[3][4][2]

Release and reception

The Zeebo version arrived in December 2010.[4]
